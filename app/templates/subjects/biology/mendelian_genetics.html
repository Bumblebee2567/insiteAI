Introduction to Classical Genetics

Classical genetics examines how heritable traits are transmitted from parents to offspring through discrete units of inheritance known as genes.

Core Genetic Principle:
Alternative forms of genes, called alleles, account for variations in inherited characters among individuals within a population.
02

Mendel's Laws of Inheritance

  • Law of Segregation: Two alleles for a heritable character segregate (separate) during gamete formation and end up in different gametes.
  • Law of Independent Assortment: Each pair of alleles segregates independently of any other pair of alleles during gamete formation, provided genes are on different chromosomes or far apart on the same chromosome.
03

Monohybrid and Dihybrid Crosses

Punnett squares are predictive grid diagrams used to calculate the expected phenotypic and genotypic ratios of genetic crosses.

  • Monohybrid Cross: Follows a single trait (e.g., flower color). Yields a classic 3:1 phenotypic ratio in the F2 generation for complete dominance.
  • Dihybrid Cross: Follows two independent traits (e.g., seed color and shape). Yields a classic 9:3:3:1 phenotypic ratio in the F2 generation.
04

Non-Mendelian & Sex-Linked Inheritance

  • Incomplete Dominance: Heterozygotes display an intermediate blended phenotype (e.g., red and white flowers producing pink offspring).
  • Codominance: Both alleles are simultaneously and fully expressed in heterozygotes (e.g., AB blood type).
  • Sex-Linked Traits: Genes located on sex chromosomes (e.g., X-linked recessive traits like hemophilia and red-green color blindness).

Exhaustive Worked Exam Examples

Problem 1 (X-Linked Recessive Inheritance):

A color-blind female marries a male with normal vision. What proportion of their sons and daughters are expected to be color-blind?

Detailed Step-by-Step Solution:
- Color blindness is an X-linked recessive trait (let allele be X^b, normal be X^B).
- Female genotype is homozygous recessive (X^b X^b). Male genotype is normal (X^B Y).
- Sons: Receive X^b from mother and Y from father → 100% color blind (X^b Y).
- Daughters: Receive X^b from mother and X^B from father → 100% carriers with normal vision (X^B X^b).
