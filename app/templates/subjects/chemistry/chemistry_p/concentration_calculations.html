The Titration Formula

When solving titration problems involving a balanced chemical equation, use the general mole relationship formula:

General Formula:
(Ca × Va) / na = (Cb × Vb) / nb
Where C = Concentration, V = Volume, n = Stoichiometric coefficient from the balanced equation, a = acid, b = base.

Dilution Formula

When preparing a diluted solution from a concentrated stock solution, the total number of moles of solute remains constant.

Dilution Equation:
C1V1 = C2V2
Where C1 and V1 are initial concentration and volume, and C2 and V2 are final concentration and volume.
04

Percentage Error & Uncertainty

Experimental apparatus always carries small calibration uncertainties (e.g., a 25 cm3 pipette has an uncertainty of ±0.06 cm3).

Percentage Error Formula:
Percentage Error = (Maximum Total Error / Measured Value) × 100%
