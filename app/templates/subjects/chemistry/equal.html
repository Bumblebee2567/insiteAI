What Is Chemical Equilibrium?

Many chemical reactions are reversible, meaning reactants form products while products simultaneously react to reform reactants (↔). Chemical Equilibrium is the state in a closed system where the rate of the forward reaction equals the rate of the reverse reaction.

A + B ⇌ C + D
Equilibrium is dynamic — macroscopic concentrations remain constant.

Forward Rate

Starts high when reactants are concentrated, then decreases over time.

Reverse Rate

Starts at zero, then increases as products accumulate.

Equilibrium State

Forward Rate = Reverse Rate. Concentrations stop changing.

The Equilibrium Constant (Kc)

The equilibrium constant (Kc) expresses the ratio of product concentrations to reactant concentrations at equilibrium, with each concentration raised to its stoichiometric coefficient.

For: aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD
Kc = ([C]c[D]d) / ([A]a[B]b)
Pure solids (s) and pure liquids (l) are omitted from Kc expressions.
Interpreting Kc:
• Kc >> 1: Products are heavily favored (equilibrium lies far right).
• Kc << 1: Reactants are heavily favored (equilibrium lies far left).
04

The Reaction Quotient (Qc)

Qc uses the exact same formula as Kc, but evaluates concentrations at any arbitrary time. Comparing Qc to Kc predicts shift direction:

  • Qc < Kc: Shifts Right (too few products).
  • Qc = Kc: At Equilibrium.
  • Qc > Kc: Shifts Left (too many products).

ICE Table Calculations

An ICE table (Initial, Change, Equilibrium) tracks concentration changes using stoichiometry.

Worked Calculation — ICE Table Example

Reaction: H₂ (g) + I₂ (g) ⇌ 2HI (g).
0.50 mol of H₂ and 0.50 mol of I₂ are mixed in a 1.0 dm³ container. At equilibrium, [HI] = 0.39 mol/dm³. Find Kc.

Step[H₂][I₂][2HI]
Initial0.500.500.00
Change−x−x+2x
Equilibrium0.3050.3050.39

Solving gives x = 0.195, leading to equilibrium concentrations [H₂] = [I₂] = 0.305 and [HI] = 0.39.

Kc = (0.39)2 / (0.305 × 0.305) = 1.635

Le Chatelier's Principle

If a dynamic equilibrium is disturbed by changing conditions, the system shifts to counteract that disturbance and re-establish equilibrium.

Solubility Product Constant (Ksp)

For sparingly soluble salts: AgCl (s) ⇌ Ag⁺ (aq) + Cl⁻ (aq)
Expression: Ksp = [Ag⁺][Cl⁻] (excluding solids).

10

Exam Checklist

  • Equilibrium means equal rates, not equal concentrations.
  • Omit pure solids and liquids from Kc and Ksp expressions.
  • Only changes in temperature alter the numerical value of Kc.
