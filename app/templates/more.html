Our Mission & Platform Vision

Insite AI is built with a singular mission: to democratize world-class academic support and transform how students prepare for high-stakes examinations. We combine rigorous, curriculum-aligned academic notes with cutting-edge artificial intelligence to provide personalized, step-by-step guidance in mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, and English language.

Whether you are preparing for the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) UTME, the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE), or foundational university courses, Insite AI delivers structured masterclasses designed for deep conceptual retention.

The Insite Standard: Every study page is structured with exhaustive step-by-step explanations, interactive simulation tools, self-assessment test boxes, and worked examination examples.

Insite AI Chat & Personal Tutoring

Stuck on a difficult past question or a complex scientific concept? The Insite AI Chat Assistant is available 24/7 to provide instant, conversational tutoring.

  • Step-by-Step Problem Solving: Ask the AI to solve mathematical and physics problems step by step.
  • Chemical Explanations: Request clear educational breakdowns of reaction mechanisms and stoichiometry.
  • Grammar & Essay Guidance: Get instant analysis on essay structure, concord corrections, and literary devices.
Seamless Integration: Every study page features a direct "Ask Insite AI" call-to-action button, allowing you to transition instantly from studying notes to chatting with your personal AI tutor.
04

Account Management: Logging In & Logging Out

Managing your Insite AI student profile is secure, fast, and straightforward.

  • Secure Registration & Login: Create your student account with your email and a secure password. Your authentication state is managed securely via Flask session cookies.
  • Personalized Workspace: Logging in unlocks your dashboard, saving your progress across subject hubs and enabling unlimited AI tutoring chat sessions.
  • Logging Out: You can safely terminate your active session at any time by clicking the Log Out button in the navigation bar. This immediately clears your secure Flask session, protecting your account privacy on shared or public devices.
